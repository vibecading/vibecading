Vibe CADing for the built and the imagined

An open innovation lab for AI-native tools in architecture, 3D, games and creative work

Join the discussion Browse the catalog

    Example prompts. Shapes are sketched in code, live in your browser

    Architects, 3D artists and game developers already describe what they want in words. vibecading connects those words to the software that does the work: Rhino, Revit, Blender, Unreal, Godot, TouchDesigner and the rest. What comes back is real geometry you can open, measure and change

    The community is the lab

    People who build with words, show their work and argue about it. Tools and products grow out of those conversations
